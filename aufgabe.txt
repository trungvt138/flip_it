Please complete the following tasks:

Conduct a usability testing
Find 5 test users
Let them do (at least 2) concrete tasks in your app (e.g. "Create a recipe" in a cooking app)
Use the following tools
Observations: Observe their actions and note down usability problems (do not help or guide them)
Think Aloud Protocol: Ask them to "think aloud" while using the app and take notes
Interview: After using the app, ask them (at least 2) open "w"-questions about the usability of your app
Analyze the data
Which problems were observed on more than 1 test user?
Which problems were mentioned by more than 1 test user?
Write a summary report
